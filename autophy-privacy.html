Autophy Privacy Policy

Autophy is an intermittent fasting tracker for Android. It keeps your data on your device. This policy explains what the app stores, how it uses Health Connect, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 31 October 2026 support@nilemnod.com

1. Summary

  • Everything stays on your device. Autophy has no account, no servers and no internet access. Nilemnod never receives your data.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Health Connect is optional. If you turn it on, weight and hydration are read from and written to Health Connect on your device only.
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

This notice applies to the Autophy Android app published by Nilemnod and takes precedence over the general Nilemnod Privacy Policy for the app.

2. Data the app stores

Autophy stores the following information in a private database on your device so that the app can work:

  • Fasting history — start and end times of your fasts, chosen plans and goals, and your fasting schedule.
  • Check-ins — how you feel during a fast (mood, energy and similar wellbeing notes) and any notes you write.
  • Body and hydration entries — weight and water intake that you log or import from Health Connect.
  • Progress — streaks, achievements and challenges calculated from your history.
  • Settings — notification, sound, unit and display preferences.

This data is used only to show your timer, history, statistics, reminders and widgets. It is not sent to Nilemnod or any third party.

3. Health Connect

If you connect Autophy to Health Connect, the app asks for permission to:

  • Read and write weight — to show your weight trend next to your fasting progress and to save weights you log in Autophy;
  • Read and write hydration — to show your water intake during fasts and to save water you log in Autophy.

Data from Health Connect is processed only on your device and is used only to provide the features described above. In line with the Limited Use requirements, this data:

  • is not transferred to Nilemnod or any third party;
  • is not used for advertising, including personalised or interest-based ads;
  • is not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • is not sold and is not read by any person.

Autophy's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can review or revoke these permissions at any time in Autophy settings or in the Health Connect app. Revoking them does not delete data already saved in Health Connect; you can manage that data in Health Connect.

4. Device permissions

  • Notifications — to tell you about fasting phases, goals, reminders and schedule events.
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms) — so that phase and goal notifications arrive on time.
  • Run at startup — to restore your reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibration — for optional haptic feedback.

None of these permissions gives the app access to personal data outside Autophy.

5. Sharing and export

Autophy never shares data automatically. Data leaves the app only when you choose to:

  • Export your fasting history as a CSV file, which you save to a location you pick or send to an app you pick;
  • Share a progress card image through the Android share sheet.

Once shared, the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

6. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Autophy's data in your device backup and restore it when you set up a new device. This backup is managed by Google under your Google account settings, not by Nilemnod. Technical data such as scheduled-alarm bookkeeping is excluded from backup.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To delete all Autophy data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Data you wrote to Health Connect stays there until you remove it in Health Connect.

Because Nilemnod does not hold any of your Autophy data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You remain in full control of it on your device.

8. Your rights

Because Nilemnod does not receive your Autophy data, you exercise your data protection rights directly in the app and on your device:

  • Access and portability — export your fasting history as a CSV file;
  • Deletion — clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app;
  • Health Connect data — review, export or delete it in the Health Connect app;
  • Withdrawing consent — revoke Health Connect permissions in Autophy settings or in Health Connect.

If you have questions, write to support@nilemnod.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the State Agency for Personal Data Protection under the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic or with the data protection authority in your country.

9. Not medical advice

Autophy provides general information about fasting phases for reference only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a doctor before fasting, especially for longer fasts or if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding.

10. Children

Autophy is not intended for children under 18, and fasting apps are not suitable for them. We do not knowingly collect data from children; in any case, the app does not send data to us.

11. Changes to this policy

If the way Autophy handles data changes, we will update this page and the "last updated" date before the change reaches the app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or Autophy: support@nilemnod.com.
