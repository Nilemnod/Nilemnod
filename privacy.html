1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Nilemnod processes personal data when you use our website nilemnod.com and our mobile apps for Android and iOS (together, the “Services”). If an app has its own privacy notice, such as Autophy, that notice takes precedence for that app.

Nilemnod is the trade name of Individual Entrepreneur Abdulkhamidov Daniiar Abdulkhamidovich (IP), registered in the Kyrgyz Republic, 12c ul. A. Karimova, Osh 723505, Kyrgyzstan. The IP is the controller responsible for your personal data.

Some apps have their own privacy notices, which take precedence over this policy for those apps:

  • Autophy Privacy Policy — intermittent fasting tracker for Android.

2. Data we collect

We aim to collect only the data we need. Depending on how you interact with us, this may include:

  • Account data — if you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile photo and Google account identifier, shared by Google with your permission. We never receive your Google password.
  • App content — progress, settings and other data you create in an app and that is synced to your account.
  • Purchase data — subscription status, product, purchase and renewal dates and transaction identifiers that we receive from the App Store or Google Play. Payments are processed by Apple or Google; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Diagnostics — crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics: error details, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier.
  • Usage data — where an app uses Google Analytics for Firebase: in-app events such as screens opened and features used, linked to a random app-instance identifier rather than your name.
  • Support data — your name, email address and the content of messages you send us through forms or by email.

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It stores two small preferences in your browser's local storage — the interface language and the light or dark theme you select. These values stay on your device, are never sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser data.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub processes technical connection data such as your IP address and browser user agent in order to deliver the page and protect the service.

Our apps do not contain advertising SDKs, do not show personalised ads and do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

5. International data transfers

We are established in the Kyrgyz Republic, and the providers listed above process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. Your data may therefore be transferred outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area.

Transfers from the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in the provider's data processing terms. Under the Law of the Kyrgyz Republic “On Personal Information”, transfers take place where they are necessary to perform the contract with you or with your consent. You can request details of these safeguards at privacy@nilemnod.com.

6. Data retention

Retention periods for each purpose are listed in section 3. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@nilemnod.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To delete your account and the data connected to it, use our account and data deletion page. It explains exactly what is deleted, what we are legally required to keep, and how long the process takes.

In the Kyrgyz Republic, the supervisory authority is the State Agency for Personal Data Protection under the Cabinet of Ministers of the Kyrgyz Republic.

We are not established in the European Union and have not appointed a representative under Article 27 GDPR, because our processing is occasional, does not include large-scale processing of special categories of data and is unlikely to result in a risk to your rights. You can always contact us directly at privacy@nilemnod.com.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work continuously to safeguard your information.

9. Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under 16 in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, or under a higher age where the law of your country requires it. Some apps set a higher minimum age — for example, Autophy is intended for adults only. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the “last updated” date. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or by email at least 30 days before they take effect.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your data, contact us at privacy@nilemnod.com or through our contact page.

This policy is written in English. Translations are provided for convenience; if there is any discrepancy, the English version prevails.
